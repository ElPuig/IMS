# Consulting a Student's Academic Data

**Required role:** Teacher (academic history) · Guidance or Coexistence coordinator (all other data)

---

## Consult a Student's Academic History

The academic history is the frozen, per-course record of a student's progress: course, study programme, group, tutor, academic result, title obtained and attendance rate. Any teacher can consult any student's history.

**From the student's file:** Educational Community → Students → *[open the student]* → **Academic history** tab.

**From its own menu:** Planning and Grading → Grades → **Academic history**. The list opens grouped by course: click a course to expand it, or type the student's name in the search box.

It is read-only. To correct a record, contact the Secretary or the Administrator.

---

## Consult Any Student's Data (Guidance and Coexistence)

Holding the **Guidance coordinator** or **Coexistence coordinator** role, you consult the data of every student in the centre, not only your own tutees':

- **Grades** — Planning and Grading → Grades → Evaluation by group and subject.
- **Academic history** — as described above.
- **Daily attendance and justifications** — Student's Attendances.
- **Attendance issues** — Student's Attendances → Issues.
- **Strikes** — Coexistence.
- **Student file** — Educational Community → Students, including the **Secretary** tab (bonifications, exemptions and enrolment authorizations).

All of it is read-only, and it does not include invoices or payments.

---

## Find a Student

Academic management → Enrollment → **Students**, then type the name in the search box. The list shows every student in the centre.
